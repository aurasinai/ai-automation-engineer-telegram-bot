# Privacy Policy

**AI Automation Engineer Telegram Bot**
Last updated: November 10, 2026

This privacy policy explains what data the AI Automation Engineer Telegram bot ("the bot") handles, why, and how. The bot is a personal portfolio project built with n8n and operated by Eko Sarwono. It is not a commercial service.

## 1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to anyone who chats with the bot on Telegram, and to the Google and Notion accounts the operator connects to the bot.

## 2. Data the bot processes

| Data | Source | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Telegram messages you send | Telegram | To understand your request and reply |
| Telegram chat ID | Telegram | To send replies and files back to the right chat |
| Document content you ask for (Word or PDF) | Generated by the AI model | To build the file you requested |
| Notion notes you ask for | Generated by the AI model | To create a note in the operator's Notion workspace |

The bot does not ask for, and is not designed to collect, passwords, payment details, government ID numbers, or other sensitive personal data. Please do not send such information to the bot.

## 3. Google user data

The bot uses the Google Drive API only to turn generated text into a Word or PDF file:

1. A temporary Google Doc is created in the operator's own Google Drive.
2. The document is exported as `.docx` or `.pdf` and sent to your Telegram chat.
3. The temporary Google Doc is deleted permanently right after.

The bot does not read, scan, or list any other files in Google Drive. It does not share Google user data with third parties, and does not use it for advertising or to train AI models.

The bot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## 4. Notion data

When you ask for a note, the bot creates a page under a single parent page in the operator's Notion workspace. It may also search and read pages in that workspace to answer your questions. The bot only accesses pages the operator has explicitly shared with its Notion integration.

## 5. Third-party services

To work, the bot sends data to these services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

- **Telegram**: message delivery
- **Groq** and **OpenRouter** (including NVIDIA Nemotron models): AI text generation. Free model endpoints may log prompts and outputs for the provider's own purposes.
- **Google Drive API**: file conversion, as described above
- **Notion API**: note creation and reading
- **n8n** (self-hosted): runs the workflow

## 6. Storage and retention

- **Conversation memory:** the last few messages per chat are kept in n8n's in-memory buffer so the bot can follow the conversation. This memory is cleared when the n8n service restarts.
- **Execution logs:** n8n keeps execution logs (including message content) for debugging. These are only accessible to the operator and are deleted periodically.
- **Files:** Word and PDF files are not kept by the bot after they are sent. They remain in your Telegram chat until you delete them.

## 7. Your choices

- You can stop using the bot at any time.
- You can delete messages and files from your Telegram chat.
- You can ask the operator to delete execution logs related to your chat by opening an issue in this repository.

## 8. Security

API keys and OAuth credentials are stored encrypted in n8n and are never included in this repository. No system is perfectly secure, so avoid sending sensitive information to the bot.

## 9. Changes

This policy may be updated when the bot changes. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version.

## 10. Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised by opening an issue in this GitHub repository.
